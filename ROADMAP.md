## Collaborative groups (S2.1)

Users create flat, invite-only workspaces with chosen colleagues. Groups get every section feature (noticeboard, whiteboard, chat, search, reminders, notifications) but have no parent, no inheritance and no hierarchy controls. The creator is the first steward. Stewards manage members, roles, name, posting policy and archive. Members can leave, and the last steward is protected. The feature shipped with P2 and reuses the shared policy module, so P3 and P4 work (sockets, board-only sessions, snapshots) covers groups automatically. Follow-ups for P5 are an optional invitation-acceptance step, group discovery or join requests if the site wants them, and group retention rules once the site sets content retention.
