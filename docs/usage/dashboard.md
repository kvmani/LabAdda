# My day

Open **My day** from navigation for active personal tasks, shared tasks assigned to
you, dated private notes, recent mentions/notices and unread conversations. Only
currently accessible source content appears. Administrators do not get a private
content overview of other people.

Task dates use the calendar timezone shown on the page. Overdue/today tags are
calendar dates, not browser UTC conversions. Lists are bounded and labeled; open
My Tasks, My Notes or the source workspace for more work. This is not a site-wide
usage report or a claim that every historical notification is loaded.

Activity/reconnect can prompt a refresh. A failed read is shown as unavailable,
not as no work. Losing a shared source removes its content while personal work
remains. Opening a notification rechecks its current source permissions.

## Alert preferences

Choose **Manage alert preferences** to control mention alerts, official-notice alerts
and scheduled note/post reminders. Your choices do not change content visibility.
Existing alerts remain available, and enabling a category does not replay skipped
reminders. Automatic task due/assignment alerts are not added in this stage.

If saving is unconfirmed or another tab changed the choices, your selections remain
visible but saving is blocked. **Reload current preferences (replaces draft)** reads
the saved choices before another edit. Settings are private to your account and
work from desktop and the mobile navigation menu.
