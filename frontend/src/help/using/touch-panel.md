## What this is for

A touch panel is a shared screen in a lab or meeting room. You can show a workspace's whiteboard on it without typing your password on a shared machine. The panel can use only that board and nothing else of yours.

## Steps

1. On your own computer, open the workspace and choose the **Whiteboard** tab.
2. Choose **Open on touch panel**.
3. A window titled **Open this board on a touch panel** shows an address and a code. Note the address and the code.
4. On the panel, open that address in the browser.
5. In **Panel code**, type the code.
6. Choose **Open board**.
7. Back on your computer, choose **Done**.
8. When you have finished at the panel, choose **End session**.

## What happens next

The board appears on the panel and the title bar says **Drawing as** with your name, so changes are recorded as yours. The panel shows no other part of LabAdda. If nobody touches it for a few minutes it locks and clears itself. A warning with a countdown appears first, and touching the screen keeps it open.

## If something goes wrong

- The code works once and expires within a couple of minutes. If it does not work, choose **Open on touch panel** again for a new code.
- If the panel says **Panel locked after inactivity.**, enter a new code to continue.
- You cannot create tasks or read your notes from the panel.
