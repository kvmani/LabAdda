## What this is for

**My day** gathers what needs your attention today from the places you can open. It shows only what you are allowed to see, and nobody else can see your page.

## Steps

1. Choose **My day** under **Personal** in the left-hand list.
2. Read **Active work and deadlines**. It lists up to 25 of your tasks, earliest due first, marked **Overdue** or **Due today** where that applies. Choose a title to open its list.
3. Read **Mentions and updates** for the latest notifications. Choose one to go to it.
4. Read **Private note deadlines** for notes of yours that have a date. Only you see these.
5. Read **Unread conversations** for chats with new messages, and choose a name to open that chat.
6. Choose **Refresh My day** to load the latest.

## What happens next

The line under the title shows the date, time zone and when the page was last checked. If something changes while the page is open, a note says **Activity may have changed**. Choose **Refresh My day** to catch up.

## If something goes wrong

- A short list is not the whole picture. Each box shows only its first few items, and says when more are available. Open **My Tasks** or **My Notes** to see the rest.
- If a box says it is unavailable, it could not be loaded. That does not mean you have nothing to do. Refresh.
- If a workspace is missing from the page, you may have lost access to it. Ask a steward.
