## What this is for

On a shared list you can say who is responsible for a task and keep the discussion with it. Assigning a task does not give anyone access they did not have before.

## Steps

1. Open the workspace and choose its **Tasks** tab. Open the list that holds the task.
2. Under the task, choose **Change assignee**.
3. Type part of a name in **Find eligible colleague** and choose **Search colleagues**.
4. In **Assignee**, choose the person, or choose **Unassigned** to clear it.
5. Choose **Save assignment**.
6. To discuss the task, choose **View comments**, type in **New task comment** and choose **Post comment**.
7. Choose **Refresh comments** to read what others have added.

## What happens next

The task shows **Assignee** with the person's name. It appears in that person's [My Tasks](my-tasks.md). The history records who changed it and when. No alert is sent when you assign a task, so tell the person yourself.

## If something goes wrong

- Only people who can currently read the workspace are offered. If someone is missing, ask a steward to add them to the workspace first.
- If the task changed while you were choosing, your choice is kept but blocked. Choose **Reload task assignment** to see the current task, then choose again.
- If you cannot see **Change assignee**, the workspace is read-only for you.
- If a comment may not have been posted, choose **Retry same comment**. Comments cannot be edited or deleted once posted.
- Private lists have no assignee because they belong only to you.
