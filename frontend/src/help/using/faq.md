## What this is for

LabAdda is a lightweight, friendly collaboration workspace designed for our research teams on the air-gapped laboratory intranet. This guide answers the most frequent questions about its purpose, scope, privacy model, and daily use.

## General questions

### Is LabAdda an official platform of our organisation?
**No.** LabAdda is **not an official platform** of our organisation. It was initiated as a small, informal internal experiment aimed at making day-to-day scientific communication, brainstorming, whiteboard sketching, and team task coordination faster and simpler without bureaucratic friction. 

Official administrative approvals, circulars, sanction orders, confidential documents, and statutory records must continue to use the organisation's designated official ERP and intranet portals.

### Who can use LabAdda, and how will it grow?
LabAdda is open to all researchers, technical officers, and team members across our laboratory units, divisions, sections, and projects. Its usage is expected to grow naturally over time as more colleagues try it out, find it helpful in their day-to-day work, and invite their project peers to collaborate.

### What kind of information should I put on LabAdda?
**LabAdda is no place for any sensitive or official data.** It is simply a digital form of your lab's whiteboard. Put only those things here which you would normally write or sketch on a physical whiteboard in your lab or office—such as brainstorming hypotheses, sketching discussion diagrams, coordinating equipment availability, and planning day-to-day next steps. All sensitive, confidential, and official records belong on designated official institutional channels.

### Can administrators read my private notes, chats, or whiteboards?
**No.** LabAdda is built with strict **administrator content blindness**. Site and unit administrators have access only to operational metadata (such as account lists, storage quotas, and server health status). They cannot see your message texts, personal note contents, private task descriptions, or whiteboard sketches.

Furthermore, personal tools (**My Whiteboard**, **My Notes**, **My Day**, and **My Calendar**) are completely isolated and private to your account.

### How does unit access work across the organisation?
Access follows our natural laboratory structure:
- **Upward-only hierarchy:** Members of a Section can see their parent Division and Group notices and events. Division members cannot see a Section's private workspaces unless they are explicitly added as Section members.
- **Projects and Private Groups:** Flat and private. Only invited members can see or participate in the group.
- **Creating new groups:** Any colleague can create a private collaborative group at any time from the left navigation bar ("+ New group") without needing IT approval.

### How does the Central Calendar work?
The Central Calendar combines schedules into one easy view:
- **Institute Events:** Announced by lab administration, visible to everyone.
- **Unit Events:** Scheduled in your Section or Division, visible to unit members.
- **Project Events:** Visible only to members of that project.
- **My Calendar:** Private to you for your personal tasks and reminders.

### Why is there a file size limit on attachments?
LabAdda runs entirely on a single intranet server with fixed storage. Keeping uploads lightweight (e.g. diagrams, micrographs, PDFs, and meeting minutes under 20 MB) ensures the server remains fast and responsive for all colleagues. For multi-gigabyte raw instrument datasets, please continue using the laboratory's dedicated central network storage (NAS).

### How can I share feedback or report a bug?
We warmly welcome your thoughts! Click the **Feedback** button in the top navigation bar at any time to share ideas, report any issues, or suggest new features. Because this is an active experiment, your feedback directly guides future updates.
