## What this is for

If a post is about something with a deadline, such as a seminar or a booking, you can add a date and a reminder time. The workspace then shows it in an upcoming list so nobody misses it.

## Steps

1. On the **Noticeboard** tab, write your post.
2. Next to **Due**, pick the date and time the thing happens.
3. Next to **Remind**, pick when you want to be reminded. Choose a time before the due time.
4. Choose **Post**.
5. To see what is coming up, look at the **Upcoming** card on the right of the noticeboard. On a narrow screen it sits below the posts.

## What happens next

The post shows its due time and reminder time, and an entry appears under **Upcoming** with the time first. At the reminder time you get a **Reminder** in your notifications, if you have left reminders switched on in [alert preferences](notifications-and-alerts.md).

## If something goes wrong

- If **Upcoming** says **Nothing scheduled.**, no post in this workspace has a due time yet.
- If you did not get a reminder, check that **Scheduled note and post reminders** is ticked in your alert preferences. Switching it on later does not replay reminders you missed.
- Times are shown in the site's time zone, which may differ from your computer's clock.
