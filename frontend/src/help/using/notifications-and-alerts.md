## What this is for

LabAdda tells you when a colleague mentions you, when an official notice is posted and when a reminder is due. You can read these in the Notifications panel and choose which kinds you want.

## Steps

1. To read alerts, choose **Notifications** in the top bar. A number beside it counts those you have not read.
2. Choose an alert to jump to the post or note it is about. Alerts show **Mentioned you**, **Reminder** or **Official notice**.
3. Choose **Mark all read** to clear the count, then **Close** to hide the panel.
4. To choose which alerts you get, open the menu with your name and choose **Alert preferences**, or choose **Alert preferences** under **Personal**.
5. Tick or untick **Mentions in posts and chat**, **Official notice alerts** and **Scheduled note and post reminders**.
6. Choose **Save alert preferences**.

## What happens next

You get only the kinds of alert that you left ticked. The choice applies to new alerts; alerts you already have stay in the panel. Switching a kind back on does not replay reminders you missed. These settings never change who can read your content.

## If something goes wrong

- If you see "That item is no longer available to you.", the post was removed or you no longer have access to its workspace.
- If saving fails or another browser tab changed your settings, your choices stay on screen but saving is blocked. Choose **Reload current preferences (replaces draft)** and make your choices again.
- Task assignments and due dates do not create alerts. See [My day](my-day.md) for what is due.
