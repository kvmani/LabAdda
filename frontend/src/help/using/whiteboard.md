## What this is for

Each workspace can have several shared whiteboards. Everyone looking at it sees changes as they happen, so you can sketch an idea or plan an experiment together.

## Before you start

You need to be allowed to write in the workspace. If you cannot, contribution tools are hidden; you can still pan, zoom and read. At parent units, immediate child stewards and their delegates contribute alongside direct members and the unit’s own stewards.

## Steps

1. Open the workspace and choose the **Whiteboard** tab.
2. Choose a colour from the row of coloured dots.
3. Choose **Pen** and drag on the board to draw.
4. Choose **Text** or **Sticky**, click where you want it, type your words in the small window and confirm.
5. Choose **Image** to add a PNG or JPEG picture from your computer. It appears in the middle of what you see.
6. Choose **Eraser** and click your own drawing, note or picture to erase it. A steward can also change other people’s items.
7. Made a mistake? Choose **Undo mine** to take back your own latest change. It never undoes other people's work.
8. To move around, choose **Pan** and drag. Use the **Zoom in** and **Zoom out** buttons or the mouse wheel to zoom, and **Fit** to see everything.

### Selecting, moving and formatting

Choose Select and click an item to select it, then drag it to a new position. For keyboard
operation, open **List view**, choose **Select item**, and use **Move left**, **Move right**,
**Move up** or **Move down**. Use **Edit selected item** (or double-click a selected note or
text label) to edit its text and appearance. You can change your own items; a current workspace
steward can also edit other members’ items.

In the editor choose **Font** and **Font size**. Choices are Sans serif, Serif, Monospace and
Handwritten (Kalam), with a small size menu from 8 to 48. Notes also have shape, colour and
size choices. Fonts are included with LabAdda and need no Internet connection. Saved styles
appear again after reloading and are used in published shared-board snapshots.

## What happens next

The word **Saved** beside the tools tells you your change is stored. Other people see it within a moment. The line beside it shows how many people are on the board and their names.

## If something goes wrong

- If it says **Saving…** for a long time or **Reconnecting…**, your connection dropped. Keep the page open; LabAdda sends your work again when it is back.
- If it says **Conflict — reloaded**, someone changed the same thing at the same time. The board has reloaded with the latest version. Redo your change if it is missing.
- Choose **List view** to read the text and sticky notes as a list, which also works with a screen reader.
- A steward can choose **Restore…** to put the board back to an earlier version. See [what a steward does](../stewards/what-a-steward-does.md).
