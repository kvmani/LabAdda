## What this is for

Every workspace has one shared whiteboard. Everyone looking at it sees changes as they happen, so you can sketch an idea or plan an experiment together.

## Before you start

You need to be allowed to write in the workspace. If you cannot, the drawing tools are greyed out and only **Pan** works, so you can still look around.

## Steps

1. Open the workspace and choose the **Whiteboard** tab.
2. Choose a colour from the row of coloured dots.
3. Choose **Pen** and drag on the board to draw.
4. Choose **Text** or **Sticky**, click where you want it, type your words in the small window and confirm.
5. Choose **Image** to add a PNG or JPEG picture from your computer. It appears in the middle of what you see.
6. Choose **Eraser** and click a drawing, note or picture to remove it.
7. Made a mistake? Choose **Undo mine** to take back your own latest change. It never undoes other people's work.
8. To move around, choose **Pan** and drag. Use the **Zoom in** and **Zoom out** buttons or the mouse wheel to zoom, and **Fit** to see everything.

## What happens next

The word **Saved** beside the tools tells you your change is stored. Other people see it within a moment. The line beside it shows how many people are on the board and their names.

## If something goes wrong

- If it says **Saving…** for a long time or **Reconnecting…**, your connection dropped. Keep the page open; LabAdda sends your work again when it is back.
- If it says **Conflict — reloaded**, someone changed the same thing at the same time. The board has reloaded with the latest version. Redo your change if it is missing.
- Choose **List view** to read the text and sticky notes as a list, which also works with a screen reader.
- A steward can choose **Restore…** to put the board back to an earlier version. See [what a steward does](../stewards/what-a-steward-does.md).
