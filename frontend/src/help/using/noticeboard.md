## What this is for

The noticeboard is where a workspace shares news, questions and announcements. Everyone who can read the workspace sees your post, and it stays there for people who join later.

## Before you start

You need to be a member of the workspace. If you see **Posting is limited to stewards in this workspace.** or a note that the workspace is archived, you can read the noticeboard but not post. Ask a steward if you need to write there.

## Steps

1. Open the workspace from the list on the left. It opens on the **Noticeboard** tab.
2. Read the line that starts with **Audience** above the box. It tells you who will see your post.
3. Click in the message box and type your update. To get a colleague's attention, type @ and their name.
4. Choose **Post**.
5. To change a post you wrote, choose **Edit** under your name, change the text and choose **Save**. Choose **Cancel** to keep the original.
6. To take one of your posts down, choose **Delete** and confirm.

## What happens next

The word **Posted** appears and your post shows at the top of the feed for everyone in the workspace. Mentioned colleagues get a notification. Older posts load when you choose **Load older posts**.

## If something goes wrong

- If posting fails, LabAdda keeps your text in the box. Check your connection and choose **Post** again.
- If you cannot find your post, it may be under **Pinned** posts at the top, or further down. Use **Load older posts**.
- Drafts are kept for each workspace while this browser tab stays open, but not after you close it.
