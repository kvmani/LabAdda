## What this is for

Your LabAdda account is created by an administrator, and you choose your own password the first time you use it. After that you sign in with your username and password.

## Before you start

You need your username and your activation code. Your administrator gives you both, usually on a printed slip. The activation code works once and stops working after a short time.

## Steps

1. Open the LabAdda address your administrator gave you in your web browser.
2. Under the **Sign in** button, choose the **activation link**.
3. In **Activation code**, type the code from your slip.
4. In **New password (at least 10 characters)**, type a password you will remember. A short sentence works well.
5. In **Confirm password**, type the same password again.
6. Choose **Activate account**.

## What happens next

LabAdda signs you in and opens your home workspace. From now on, type your username in **Username**, your password in **Password** and choose **Sign in**.

## If something goes wrong

- If you see "Passwords do not match.", type the same password in both boxes and choose **Activate account** again.
- If LabAdda says the code is not valid or has expired, ask your administrator for a new activation code.
- If you forget your password later, ask your administrator to reset it. Nobody can read your password, so it cannot be looked up.
