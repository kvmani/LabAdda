## What this is for

A group is a private workspace for you and the colleagues you choose, from any section or project. It has its own noticeboard, whiteboard, chat and tasks. A private conversation is a group of just two people that opens straight on chat.

## Steps

1. In the left-hand list, under **My groups**, choose **+ New group**. For a one-to-one chat, choose **+ Private conversation** instead.
2. In **Group name**, type a name. A private conversation can leave this blank.
3. If you like, describe the group in **Purpose (optional)**.
4. In **Find colleagues**, type part of a name or username, then choose a name from the list. For a private conversation choose one person.
5. Check the names under the box. Remove someone with the cross button next to their name (its name starts with **Remove**).
6. Choose the **Create group** button. It also shows how many colleagues you picked.

## What happens next

The new group opens and appears under **My groups**. You are its steward. Only listed members can see anything in it. Parent units and administrators cannot, unless they are members too. See [what a steward does](../stewards/what-a-steward-does.md) to add people later.

## If something goes wrong

- If the screen says the creation may have succeeded, look under **My groups** before trying again, so you do not create a second copy.
- If a colleague does not appear in the list, check the spelling of their name or username. Only active people are listed.
- If you add the wrong person, a steward can remove them from the **Members** tab. They can no longer open the group, but they may have read earlier content while they were a member.
