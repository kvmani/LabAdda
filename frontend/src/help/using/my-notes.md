## What this is for

**My Notes** is your private scratchpad for things like a protocol reminder or a thought for a meeting. Only you can see these notes. They do not appear in workspace search, in colleagues' notifications or on a shared touch panel, and administrators cannot read them.

## Steps

1. Choose **My Notes** under **Personal** in the left-hand list.
2. In **Title**, type a short heading. It can be left empty.
3. In **Note**, type the text.
4. If it has a deadline, pick a date and time next to **Due**. To be reminded, pick a time next to **Remind me**.
5. Choose **Add note**.
6. To change a note, choose **Edit**, change it and choose **Save note**. Choose **Cancel** to leave it as it was.
7. To remove a note, choose **Delete** and confirm.

## What happens next

The note appears in your list. If it has a due date it also shows under **Private note deadlines** on [My day](my-day.md). At the reminder time you get a notification, if reminders are switched on in [alert preferences](notifications-and-alerts.md).

## If something goes wrong

- If a note does not save, your text stays in the form. Check your connection and choose **Add note** again.
- If you do not get reminders, check **Scheduled note and post reminders** in your alert preferences.
- **Delete** takes the note out of your list. Copy its text first if you may want it again.
- If **No notes yet.** shows after you saved one, refresh the page.
