## What this is for

LabAdda has the same layout on every screen, so once you know where things are you can find them anywhere. This page shows you the three places to look.

## Steps

1. Look at the top bar. **Help** opens this guide, **Notifications** shows what has changed for you, and the menu with your name has **Sign out**.
2. Look at the list on the left. Under **Personal** you will find **My day**, **My Tasks**, **My To-do Lists**, **My Notes**, **Alert preferences** and **My storage usage**. Only you can see these.
3. Further down the list are the units, projects and groups you belong to. Choose one to open its workspace.
4. At the top of a workspace there is a row of tabs: **Noticeboard**, **Whiteboard**, **Chat**, **Tasks**, **Members** and **Storage**. Choose a tab to change what you see.
5. On a small screen the left-hand list becomes a drop-down called **Switch workspace**.

## What happens next

Whatever you choose stays open until you choose something else. Your place in each workspace is not lost when you move between them.

## If something goes wrong

- If a workspace you expect is missing from the list, you have not been added to it. Ask the steward of that workspace to add you.
- If the page says **No workspaces yet**, you have not been added to any workspace. Ask an administrator, or choose **Create a group** to start one with colleagues.
- If the top bar says **Offline**, your connection to the server has dropped. Check your network cable or Wi-Fi. LabAdda reconnects by itself.
