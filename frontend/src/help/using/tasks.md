## What this is for

To-do lists keep track of what needs doing. Lists under **My To-do Lists** are private to you. Lists on a workspace's **Tasks** tab are shared with everyone who can read that workspace. Both work the same way.

## Steps

1. Choose **My To-do Lists** in the left-hand list, or open a workspace and choose its **Tasks** tab.
2. In **New list title**, type a name and choose **Create list**. Choose a list's name to open it.
3. Choose **New task**.
4. Fill in **Task title**. Add a **Description**, a **Due date** and a **Priority** (low, normal or high) if you want them.
5. To break the task into steps, choose **Add checklist item** and type each step. Tick a step when it is done.
6. Choose **Save task**.
7. To change a task later, choose its **Edit task** button, make your changes and choose **Save task** again.
8. When it is finished, choose **Edit task**, tick **Task completed** and choose **Save task**. Untick it to reopen the task.

## What happens next

The task shows its state, priority and due date in the list. Use **Workflow state** to move it between **To do**, **In progress**, **Blocked** and **Done**. To see who changed what, choose **View task history**. Editing never erases earlier values from the history.

## If something goes wrong

- If you see "This workspace is read-only", you can read the tasks but not change them.
- If a colleague saved the same task first, your changes stay on screen. Copy anything you want to keep, then choose **Load latest task (replaces draft)**.
- If the screen says a save may not have gone through, choose **Retry same task creation** instead of filling the form again.
- Choose **Refresh tasks** or **Refresh lists** to see new work. Choose **More tasks** to see further down a long list.
- Drafts are kept only while the page stays open.
- Due dates do not send reminders. To see what is due, open [My day](my-day.md).
