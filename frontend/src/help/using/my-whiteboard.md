## What this is for

My Whiteboard is your private space for sketches and sticky notes. Other members and
administrators cannot read it. It is separate from shared workspace whiteboards and My Notes.

## Steps

1. Open **My Whiteboard** from the Personal navigation or your home page.
2. Choose **Draw**, choose a pen colour and draw with a mouse, pen or finger.
3. Choose **Sticky** and click on the canvas to place a note.
4. Choose **Select** to choose a note and edit its text, shape, size or colour, or drag to position it.
5. Choose **Save** and wait for the saved status before leaving.
6. Use **Undo** to remove the most recently added object from your draft.

## What happens next

The saved board is available when you sign in again. Saves retain earlier revisions on the
server. No team notifications or shared-board events include your private content.

## If something goes wrong

A failed save keeps your draft on the page. If another tab saved first, the app refuses to
replace its work. Board limits are 500 objects and 20,000 drawing
points. Unsaved board changes survive links within the app in this signed-in tab. Save before closing
or signing out; browser reloads warn about unsaved work.
