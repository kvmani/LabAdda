## What this is for

The central **Calendar** displays schedules and activities across the organisation. Events follow LabAdda's access rules:
- **Institute Events**: Site-wide seminars and holidays visible to all staff.
- **Unit Events**: Scheduled at Division, Group or Section level. A Section member sees events from their Section and parent Division. Division-only staff do not see Section events.
- **Private Groups and Projects**: Flat, private calendars visible only to members.
- **My Calendar**: Your personal calendar, completely private to you and invisible to administrators.

All times are displayed in Indian Standard Time (IST).

## Steps

1. Select **Calendar** under **Personal** in the navigation bar.
2. Switch between **Month**, **Week**, **Day** and **Schedule** views using the top-right view switcher.
3. Use the **<** and **>** buttons or choose **Today** to navigate dates.
4. Filter calendar layers in the left sidebar under **My Calendars** by ticking or unticking layers.
5. Choose **+ Create** to schedule an event:
   - Enter title, target scope (Personal, Site, or workspace), dates, times, location, and description.
   - Choose a color code to keep your schedule organized.
   - Choose **Save**.
6. Select any event chip to view details, update information or delete the event if you have permission.
