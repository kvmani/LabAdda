## What this is for

Search finds words in the posts and chat messages of one workspace. It looks only where you are allowed to read, so you will never see results from a workspace you cannot open.

## Steps

1. Open the workspace you want to search.
2. On the **Noticeboard** tab, find the card whose heading starts with **Search**.
3. Type at least two letters in the box marked **Posts and chat**.
4. Pause for a moment. Results appear by themselves; use the clear button in the box to start again.

## What happens next

A line shows how many posts and how many chat messages matched, followed by the matching text. Posts have a note icon and chat messages a speech bubble. Search covers one workspace at a time. Your private notes are never included.

## If something goes wrong

- If nothing happens, you typed fewer than two letters.
- If you cannot find something you remember, check you are in the right workspace, or search a word that is likely to appear in it. Search matches words as typed.
- To look for a post you know is old, you can also use **Load older posts** on the noticeboard.
