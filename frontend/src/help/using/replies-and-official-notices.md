## What this is for

Replies keep a conversation together under the post it is about. An official notice is a post a steward marks as important, so that people can tell it from ordinary discussion.

## Steps

1. Find the post you want to answer on the **Noticeboard** tab.
2. Click in the box that says **Write a reply…** at the bottom of the post and type your answer.
3. Choose **Reply**.
4. To spot official notices, look for the **Official notice** label above the post. Stewards also choose **Pin** to keep a post at the top, where it is labelled **Pinned**.
5. To remove a reply you wrote, choose **Delete** next to it.

## What happens next

Your reply appears under the post straight away, with your name and the time. Official notices send an alert to workspace members unless they have turned that off in [alert preferences](notifications-and-alerts.md).

## If something goes wrong

- If there is no reply box, the workspace is read-only or archived. Ask a steward.
- If your reply does not appear, check your connection and try again. Your text stays in the box until it is saved.
- Only stewards can mark a post as an official notice. If you need one, ask a steward. They can follow [pinning and official notices](../stewards/pinning-and-official-notices.md).
