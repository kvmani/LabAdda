## What this is for

Use **Suspend access** when a person's access must stop immediately. Use **Reinstate** to bring back a person who was suspended, retired or left: they get a new activation code, but none of their earlier access returns by itself.

## Before you start

You need to be a site administrator. You cannot suspend yourself or the last administrator. Have a reason ready; it is required.

## Steps

1. Open **People**, select the person, and on the **Record** tab choose **Suspend access**.
2. Read the review: positions they hold end, and units where they are the only steward are listed.
3. Type the **Reason for this change** and confirm, then choose **Done**.

To reinstate:

1. Open the person's profile and choose **Reinstate**.
2. Read the review. It lists where they worked before as a starting point; nothing is given back automatically.
3. Type the **Reason for this change** and confirm. Print or copy the activation slip that is shown once.
4. Use Transfer or **Give additional charge** to give the access they need now.

## What happens next

A suspended person is signed out everywhere at once and cannot sign in. Their memberships stay on record but give no access. On reinstatement their status becomes invited, earlier memberships, positions and any administrator role are removed, old codes and the old password stop working, and they activate the account with the new code.

## If something goes wrong

- "Ask another administrator to make this change to your own account". You cannot suspend yourself.
- "Only suspended, retired or departed people can be reinstated". The person still has access.
- The activation slip was lost. Issue a password reset from the basic account tools.
