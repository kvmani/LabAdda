## What this is for

Use this to add a unit, to rename or recode one, or to move a unit with all its sub-units under a different unit. A unit keeps its identity when renamed or moved, so links and records keep working.

## Before you start

You need to be a site administrator. Every unit has a short **Unit code** of 2 to 24 capital letters, digits or dashes, such as SSD-TF, and each code is used once. The top unit cannot be moved, merged, split or closed.

## Steps

1. Open **Administration**, choose **Organisation** and choose the unit in the chart.
2. To add a unit below it, choose **Add sub-unit**. Type the **Unit name** and **Unit code**. Optionally choose a **Head of the new unit (optional)** from the people of the parent unit. Choose **Review change**.
3. To rename, choose **Rename or recode**, change the **Unit name** or **Unit code**, and choose **Review change**.
4. To move, choose **Move to…**, choose **Move under**, and choose **Review change**. You can also drag the unit box onto its new parent.
5. Read **What will happen** and **Things to check**. For a move, each line says how many people will be able to read, or will no longer be able to read, a unit above; only counts are shown.
6. Type the **Reason for this change** and confirm. Choose **Done**.

## What happens next

The unit appears or changes in the chart at once. People who lose or gain access see their pages update straight away. The change, with your reason, is recorded in the audit trail.

## If something goes wrong

- "A unit cannot move beneath itself or its own sub-units". Choose a unit that is not inside the one you are moving.
- "The code is already used by another unit". Choose a different code; closed units keep theirs.
- "Someone changed this record or its consequences". A fresh review is shown; read it and confirm again.
- The person chosen as Head is not active. Choose someone else, or leave the Head empty and appoint one later.
