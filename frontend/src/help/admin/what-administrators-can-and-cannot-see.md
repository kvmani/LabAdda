## What this is for

Being an administrator lets you look after accounts, units and the health of the site. It never lets you read anyone's work. You can use this page to answer a colleague who asks "can the administrator read my messages?".

## Steps

1. Open **Administration** and look at any page. You will see names, counts, sizes, times and statuses.
2. Use **People** to see who has an account, their home unit, status and positions.
3. Use **Audit** to see who changed what and when, with the reason that person typed.
4. Use **Storage** to see how much space each workspace uses, as a size and a count.

## What happens next

You never see message text, note titles, file names, whiteboard images, task text or what people typed into search. Being an administrator does not make you a member of any unit, project or group, so you cannot open their workspaces. To work in a unit you must be added to it like anyone else, and that change appears in the audit trail.

## If something goes wrong

- Someone asks you to read a message or recover a note. Administrators cannot do this. Tell them to ask the steward of the workspace.
- A page seems to show private text. Do not copy it. Tell the person who looks after the server at once.
