## What this is for

A unit administrator looks after the people and units of one branch of the organisation: the unit they were appointed to and every unit below it. This page explains what you can do and what you will be asked to hand to a site administrator.

## Steps

1. Open **Administration**. You will see only **People** and **Organisation**.
2. In **People**, search and filter. The list holds only people whose home unit is in your branch.
3. Open a person to add them, edit their record, transfer them between units of your branch, change their role or position, record leave or retirement, or give a new activation code. Each change is reviewed first, as for a site administrator.
4. In **Organisation**, choose a unit below the one you administer to add a sub-unit, rename it, move it, merge it or close it.
5. Always type a clear **Reason for this change**. It is kept in the audit trail with your name.

## What happens next

Changes take effect as reviewed and are recorded in the audit trail. If your appointment is withdrawn or your branch shrinks before you confirm a review, the change is refused and nothing is written. A change scheduled for a later day is checked again that day and held for review if it no longer fits.

## If something goes wrong

- A person or unit is not found. It is outside your branch. You cannot see it, even though it exists.
- A review says "This change also reaches people, units or groups outside your units". Moving someone into or out of your branch, or removing them from a project or group, needs a site administrator. If you are appointed to both branches you can move people between them yourself.
- You cannot change another unit administrator whose branch is larger than yours.
- **Import staff list**, **Needs attention**, **Basic account tools** and appointments are for site administrators only.
- You cannot read posts, chat, notes, files or whiteboards in your branch unless you are a member of that unit like anyone else. The role gives no such access.
