## What this is for

Use this when someone covers a position for a limited time, for example Asha acting as Head of the Surface Science Division for 60 days after Dana retires. You choose the end date; LabAdda ends the appointment on that date without anyone having to remember.

## Before you start

You need to be a site administrator. Know the end date. An acting Head can be appointed while the permanent Head is away on leave; in that case the Head keeps the position and the acting Head covers it.

## Steps

1. Open **People**, select the person who will act, and on the **Record** tab choose **Appoint to a position**.
2. Choose the **Unit** and the **Position**, tick **Acting appointment** and choose **Acting until**.
3. Choose **Review change**. The plan shows the date and the role the person returns to afterwards.
4. Type the **Reason for this change** and confirm, then choose **Done**.

You can also choose an acting successor while recording a retirement (**Retire or leave**) or a long leave (**Record long leave**).

## What happens next

At the start of the end date the appointment ends automatically and the person's role in that unit returns to what it was before: a member stays a member, and someone who was not a member loses the membership given for the appointment. The ending is recorded on their timeline so that a permanent decision can be made.

## If something goes wrong

- "already has an acting Head". End or change that appointment first.
- "Choose the end date of the acting appointment after today". Pick a later date, within five years.
- If the acting person should stay on, appoint them permanently before the end date.
