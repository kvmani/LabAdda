## What this is for

Use this when someone is promoted, for example when Chen in Catalysis becomes a Scientific Officer D and takes over as steward of the section. The review says plainly whether the person gains or loses the power to moderate a unit.

## Before you start

You need to be a site administrator. The person must already be a member of the unit whose role changes; to give access in another unit, use an additional charge instead. To replace an existing Head, use **Appoint to a position**.

## Steps

1. Open **People** and select the person to open their profile.
2. On the **Record** tab choose **Promote or change role**.
3. Change the **Designation** if it changes. To change a role, choose the unit under **Change the role in**, then the **New role**. A steward can also be given a vacant **Position in this unit**.
4. Choose **Review change** and read **What will happen** and **Things to check**.
5. Type the **Reason for this change** and choose the button that names the person.
6. Choose **Done**.

## What happens next

The designation and role change at once. A new steward can moderate the unit straight away; a person who stops being a steward loses those powers at once. Any position is listed under **Positions** on the profile and in the directory.

## If something goes wrong

- "holds a position". A Head or Deputy must stay a steward. End the position first with **Appoint to a position** for someone else.
- "would have no active steward". Make another member a steward first.
- "already has a Head". Use **Appoint to a position** to replace the Head.
