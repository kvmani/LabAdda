## What this is for

Use this when two units become one, when part of a unit becomes a unit of its own, or when a unit is no longer used. Nothing is deleted: a merged or closed unit stays in the system as read-only history.

## Before you start

You need to be a site administrator. The top unit cannot be merged, split or closed. A unit can only be closed when no person has it as their home unit and it has no open sub-units.

## Steps

1. Open **Administration**, choose **Organisation** and choose the unit.
2. To merge, choose **Merge into…**, choose **Merge into** the other unit, and choose **Review change**. People whose home is this unit get the other unit as their home and become its members. Its positions end, its sub-units move under the other unit, and the unit is closed.
3. To split, choose **Split off a new unit…**, type the **New unit name** and **New unit code**, tick the people who move, and choose **Review change**. The new unit appears beside the original, under the same parent.
4. To close an empty unit choose **Close unit**; to bring a closed one back choose **Reopen unit**. Tick **Show closed units** to find closed units in the chart.
5. Read **Problems to fix first** and **Things to check**. If the split would leave a unit without a steward, tick **I understand a unit will be left with no steward** or go back and change who moves.
6. Type the **Reason for this change** and confirm. Choose **Done**.

## What happens next

Former members keep read access to a merged or closed unit's history; no one can add to it. Anyone who loses sight of a unit sees their page update at once. Open shared tasks assigned to people who move stay where they are, and the review gives their number so the unit's stewards can reassign them.

## If something goes wrong

- "Transfer them first". Someone still has the unit as their home unit. Transfer them, or merge the unit instead.
- "Move or close them first". The unit still has open sub-units.
- "The unit would have no active steward". Tick the acknowledgement or appoint a steward before splitting.
- "Someone changed this record or its consequences". Read the fresh review and confirm again.
