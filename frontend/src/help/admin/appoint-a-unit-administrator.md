## What this is for

A large division is easier to run when someone who knows it can add people, record transfers and change roles there without waiting for a site administrator. A unit administrator can do that for the unit they are appointed to and the units below it, and nowhere else. The appointment gives no access to anyone's posts, chat, notes, files or whiteboard.

## Before you start

Only a site administrator can appoint or withdraw a unit administrator. The person must be active and must not already be a site administrator. A unit administrator cannot appoint anyone, including themselves.

## Steps

1. Open **Administration**, choose **People**, and select the person to open their profile.
2. On the **Record** tab choose **Make unit administrator**.
3. Choose the **Unit to administer** and choose **Next**.
4. Read **What will happen**, type the **Reason for this change** and choose **Appoint unit administrator**.
5. To see everyone who has been appointed, open **Organisation** and read **Unit administrators**.
6. To end an appointment, choose **Withdraw** beside the name, review the change and confirm it with a reason.

## What happens next

The appointment starts at once and is written to the audit trail with your reason. The person sees **People** and **Organisation** in their **Administration** menu, filtered to their branch. Withdrawing the appointment removes both pages on their next click; a change they had already reviewed can no longer be applied.

## If something goes wrong

- **Make unit administrator** is missing. The person is not active, or is already a site administrator.
- The review says the person already administers that unit. Nothing needs doing.
- A unit administrator reports a change is refused. Changes that touch another branch, a project or a group need a site administrator, or a second appointment for the other branch.
