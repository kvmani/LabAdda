## What this is for

Use this when someone joins and needs to sign in to LabAdda. You record their details, choose their home unit and role, review what will happen, and hand them a printed activation slip.

## Before you start

You need to be a site administrator. Have the person's full name, a username for them, and their home unit ready. The username can never be changed later. The employee number, designation, work email and phone extension are optional.

## Steps

1. Open **Administration**, choose **People**, then choose **Add person**.
2. Type the **Full name** and the **Username**. Fill in the optional fields you have. Leave **Start date** empty for today. Choose **Next**.
3. Choose the **Home unit** and the **Role in the home unit**: Member, or Steward if they will look after the unit. Choose **Review change**.
4. Read **What will happen**. If **Problems to fix first** appears, choose **Back** and correct the details. Read **Things to check**: a very similar name may mean the person already has an account.
5. Type the **Reason for this change**, for example "New joiner in Corrosion", then choose **Add person**.
6. The **LabAdda activation slip** appears. Choose **Print activation slip**, or copy the code by hand, then choose **Done**.

## What happens next

The person's account exists with the status Invited and they are a member of their home unit. They open the sign-in address on the slip, enter the code and choose their own password before it expires. The code is shown only once: after you close the dialog nobody can see it again, not even an administrator. The change, with your reason, appears on the person's **Timeline**. Creating an account never makes someone an administrator and gives you no access to their work.

## If something goes wrong

- "That username is already in use". Choose another username.
- "That employee number is already assigned to another person". Check the number; two people cannot share one.
- The home unit cannot be chosen. Projects, groups and archived units cannot be a home unit.
- "Someone changed this record or its consequences". A fresh plan is shown; read it again and apply.
- The code was lost, or it expired before the person used it. Open their profile, choose **Issue a new activation code**, give a reason and choose **Issue new code**. A new slip appears; any earlier unused code stops working. This is only offered while the person is Invited.
- The confirmation did not arrive. Reload the people list: if the person is there, the account was created; issue a new activation code for them rather than adding them again.
