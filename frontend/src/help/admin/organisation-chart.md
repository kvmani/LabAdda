## What this is for

Use the chart to see how the organisation is arranged and to start changes to units and people. It shows names, codes, roles and counts only. It never shows anyone's messages, notes, tasks or files, and being an administrator does not give you access to them.

## Before you start

You need to be a site administrator. Nothing you do on the chart is saved at once: every gesture opens a review that lists the consequences first.

## Steps

1. Open **Administration** and choose **Organisation**.
2. Each box is a unit: its code, name, Head and the number of people whose home it is. Choose the small **+** or **−** on a box, or press Space on it, to show or hide the units below. **Collapse all** and **Expand all** do this for the whole chart.
3. Use **Zoom in**, **Zoom out** and **Fit to screen** to change the size. Tick **Show closed units** to include units that have been closed.
4. Type in **Find a unit or person** to search by unit name, unit code or person name. Choose a result to open the chart at that unit.
5. Choose a unit to open its panel: Head, stewards, sub-units and people, with every action for the unit.
6. To start a change by dragging, press on a person's name in the panel, or on a unit box, and release it over another unit. A person dropped on a unit opens the transfer review; a unit dropped on another opens the move review. Press Escape while dragging to cancel.
7. The same changes are available without dragging: **Transfer** beside each person, and **Move to…** in the panel.
8. With the keyboard: Tab to the chart, then use the arrow keys (Up goes to the unit above, Down to the first unit below, Left and Right to neighbours), Enter to open a unit and Space to hide or show its sub-units.

## What happens next

Projects and groups are listed beside the chart with their stewards and member counts. They are not part of the tree. Any change you start goes through the review step described in the pages below.

## If something goes wrong

- "Staff information is unavailable". Choose **Try again**.
- "Your administration access changed". Reload the page; your access was ended or reduced.
- A unit you expect is missing. It may be closed: tick **Show closed units**.
