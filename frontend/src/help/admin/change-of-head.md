## What this is for

Use this when the leadership of a unit changes. There is one current Head per unit. The successor always becomes a steward of the unit, and the review shows what happens to the person who held the position before.

## Before you start

You need to be a site administrator. The successor must be active and belong to the unit: their home unit is the unit or one of its sub-units, or they are already a member. If they work elsewhere, transfer them or give them an additional charge first.

## Steps

1. Open **People** and select the successor to open their profile.
2. On the **Record** tab choose **Appoint to a position**.
3. Choose the **Unit** and the **Position**.
4. For a Head, choose what **The current Head stays as**: steward or member. To move the outgoing Head elsewhere or record a retirement, use Transfer or **Retire or leave** afterwards.
5. Leave **Acting appointment** unticked for a permanent appointment. See the acting appointments page for the other case.
6. Choose **Review change**, read the plan, type the **Reason for this change** and confirm.
7. Choose **Done**.

## What happens next

The outgoing holder's position ends and their role changes as chosen. The successor is listed under **Positions** on their profile and in the people directory. Both changes appear on the people's timelines.

## If something goes wrong

- "does not belong to". Transfer the successor or give an additional charge, then appoint them.
- "is not active and cannot hold a position". Only active people can hold a position.
- "already holds this position". Nothing needs to change.
