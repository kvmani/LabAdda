## What this is for

Use this when a person helps in another unit as well as their own, for example a member of Catalysis who also looks after Thin Films for a month. Their home unit does not change.

## Before you start

You need to be a site administrator. The person must be active or on leave and must not already be a member of the second unit (to change an existing role, use **Promote or change role**).

## Steps

1. Open **People**, select the person, and on the **Record** tab choose **Give additional charge**.
2. Choose the **Second unit** and the **Role in the second unit**. Optionally choose a **Position in the second unit**.
3. Optionally choose **Ends on**: on that date the access is removed automatically.
4. Choose **Review change**, read which units they will be able to read, type the **Reason for this change** and confirm.
5. Choose **Done**.

## What happens next

The person can read and take part in the second unit at once. The charge is listed under **Positions** on their profile. If you chose an end date, the membership is removed automatically at the start of that day and recorded on their timeline.

## If something goes wrong

- "is already a member". Use **Promote or change role** instead.
- "This is already the person's home unit". Choose a different unit.
- "already has a Head". Choose Deputy, or replace the Head with **Appoint to a position**.
