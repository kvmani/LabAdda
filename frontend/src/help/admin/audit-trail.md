## What this is for

The **Audit** page is the permanent record of changes to people, units, positions and settings. Each line says who did it, what happened and the reason they gave. It shows no private work.

## Steps

1. Open **Administration** and choose **Audit**.
2. Narrow the list with **From date**, **To date**, **Done by**, **About person**, **Unit**, **Kind of change** and **Outcome**. Type words from a reason in **Search reasons**.
3. Read the **When**, **Done by**, **Kind**, **What happened** and **Outcome** columns.
4. Choose a line in **What happened** to open the **Audit entry**. It shows the **Stated reason**, then **Before and after** values and **Recorded details**.
5. Choose **Export matching entries** to download the filtered list.
6. Choose **Older entries** to go back in time and **Newest entries** to return. **Clear audit filters** removes every filter.

## What happens next

Entries cannot be edited or deleted. A change that was refused is also recorded, with its outcome.

## If something goes wrong

- "No audit entries match these filters". Choose **Clear audit filters**.
- A line says Operator tooling. The change was made from the server by the person who looks after it, not on screen.
