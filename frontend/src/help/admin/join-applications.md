## What this is for

Colleagues who do not yet have an account can choose **Apply to join LabAdda** on the sign-in page and send their name, preferred username, unit and contact details. Nothing is created until a site administrator reviews the application here.

## Before you start

You need to be a site administrator. Have the office staff list to hand so you can confirm the person's name, employee number and unit.

## Steps

1. Open **Administration**, choose **People**, then the **Join Applications** tab. The number beside it counts applications waiting for you.
2. **Pending Review** lists new applications. Check each against your records.
3. To accept one, choose **Review, Verify & Approve**. Correct the name, employee number, designation or unit if your records differ, then choose **Confirm & Issue Activation Code**.
4. The activation code appears once. Copy it and give it to the person privately, then choose **Done**.
5. To refuse one, choose **Decline…**, type a short note for the record and choose **Decline Application**.

## What happens next

An approved person gets an Invited account as a member of the chosen unit and sets their own password with the code. The applicant always sees the same thank-you message, whether or not their username or employee number is already used: this stops the public form being used to find out who works here. Applicants can only choose divisions and sections; private groups and projects are never shown to them and are never chosen on approval.

## If something goes wrong

- "Another account already uses this username". Decline the application with a note and contact the applicant, using the details they gave, to choose another username.
- "A staff record with employee number … already exists". The person may already have an account: check **People** before doing anything else.
- The same person applied twice. Approve one application and decline the other.
- The code was lost. Open the person's profile under **People** and issue a new activation code.
