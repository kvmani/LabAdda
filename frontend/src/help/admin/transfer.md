## What this is for

Use this when a person moves from one unit to another, for example from Thin Films to Catalysis. LabAdda shows exactly which memberships change, which units they gain or lose sight of, and how many open tasks need a new owner, before anything is saved.

## Before you start

You need to be a site administrator. Only invited, active or on-leave people can be transferred. Know the new unit and the role they will have there. A handover period, where access to the old unit ends on a later date, is not available yet: keep the old unit as additional access and remove it later instead.

## Steps

1. Open **Administration**, choose **People**, and select the person to open their profile.
2. On the **Record** tab choose **Transfer to another unit**.
3. Choose the **New home unit** and the **Role in the new unit**, then choose **Next**.
4. Decide on the options. Tick **Keep access to** the old unit only if they should stay a member there as well. Under **Projects and groups to keep**, untick any they should leave. Choose **Review change**.
5. Read **What will happen**, with each value before and after, and **Things to check**: units they will be able to read, units they will no longer read, and the number of open shared tasks assigned to them in units they will lose.
6. If the old unit, or the new unit when you lower their role there, would be left without a steward, tick **I understand a unit will be left with no steward**, or cancel and appoint another steward first.
7. Type the **Reason for this change** and choose the **Transfer** button, which repeats the person's name.
8. Read the summary and choose **Done**.

## What happens next

The person's home unit and memberships change at once. Any page they had open for a unit they lost stops updating and shows that it is no longer available. Stewards of each unit they lost where they still had open shared tasks receive a notice, **Tasks to reassign**, naming the person and the number of tasks; the task titles are never shown to you. The change, with your reason, appears on the person's **Timeline**.

## If something goes wrong

- "Someone changed this record or its consequences". Another change happened since you reviewed; a fresh plan is shown, read it and apply again.
- A project or group cannot be left because it would have no steward. Keep that membership, or ask its members to choose a new steward first.
- "The person already has this home unit". Choose a different unit.
- "The change could not be confirmed". Reload the person's record to see whether it was applied before trying again.
