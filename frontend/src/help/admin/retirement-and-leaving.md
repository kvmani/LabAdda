## What this is for

Use this when a person retires or leaves the organisation, for example when Dana, Head of the Surface Science Division, retires. The review lists every workspace where they are the last steward and says who takes over before anything changes.

## Before you start

You need to be a site administrator. The change takes effect today. Decide who takes over each position and each project or group where the person is the only steward. A project or group successor must be one of its own members.

## Steps

1. Open **People**, select the person, and on the **Record** tab choose **Retire or leave**.
2. Choose the **Reason for leaving**. Under **Memberships to keep on record**, tick any memberships that should stay listed; by default all are removed.
3. Choose **Review change**. The plan lists the positions that end and the workspaces they are the last steward of.
4. Under **Who takes over**, choose a successor for each one. For a position you can tick **Acting appointment** and choose **Acting until**. For a unit you may instead tick **I understand a unit may be left without a Head or steward**.
5. Read **Things to check**, including the number of open shared tasks per workspace, type the **Reason for this change** and confirm.
6. Choose **Done**.

## What happens next

The person can no longer sign in and every open session ends at once. Their posts, messages and task history stay and keep their name. Successors take over straight away. Stewards of each workspace with open tasks assigned to the person receive a notice, **Tasks to reassign**, naming the person and the number of tasks. Their personal notes stay private to everyone.

## If something goes wrong

- "At least one active administrator must remain". Appoint another administrator first.
- "Choose the new steward of ... from its active members". Pick someone who is already in that project or group.
- "Choose who takes over". Choose a successor or tick the acknowledgement.
