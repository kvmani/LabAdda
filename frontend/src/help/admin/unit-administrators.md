## What this is for

Every unit should have a Head or a steward who can look after its people and posts. The unit panel shows who they are, so you can spot a unit nobody looks after.

## Steps

1. Open **Administration**, choose **Organisation** and choose a unit.
2. Read **Head**, **Acting head** and **Stewards** in the panel. **None appointed** next to **Head** means the position is vacant.
3. To make someone a steward, open **People**, select them and choose **Promote or change role**.
4. To appoint or replace a Head, select the person and choose **Appoint to a position**.
5. To cover a Head who is away, use an acting appointment.

## What happens next

The panel changes as soon as the review is confirmed. The **Needs attention** list on **People** names units with no steward or Head.

## If something goes wrong

- A unit has no steward. Its members cannot ask anyone to moderate it. Promote a member or appoint a Head today.
- A person you want is not listed. They must belong to the unit first; transfer them in.
