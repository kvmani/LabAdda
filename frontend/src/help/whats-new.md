## What this is for

A short tour of what LabAdda offers in version 1.0. You see a reminder about this page once for each new release. Follow the links to learn how each part works.

## What is new

- *Noticeboard.* Post notices for a unit, reply, pin an official notice and attach files. See [the noticeboard](using/noticeboard.md) and [replies and official notices](using/replies-and-official-notices.md).
- *Whiteboard.* Draw together in real time, and publish a snapshot to the noticeboard. See [the whiteboard](using/whiteboard.md) and [publishing a snapshot](using/publishing-a-snapshot.md).
- *Chat.* Talk with the people in a space, answer in threads and share files. See [chat and threads](using/chat-and-threads.md) and [private groups](using/private-groups.md).
- *Tasks.* Keep task lists, give a task to a colleague, and turn a chat message into a task. See [tasks](using/tasks.md) and [assigning tasks](using/assigning-tasks.md).
- *My day.* One page that gathers your tasks, dates, mentions and unread chats. See [reading My day](using/my-day.md).
- *Private notes.* A place for your own notes that nobody else can open, even an administrator. See [your notes](using/my-notes.md).
- *Search.* Find posts, chat and tasks you are allowed to see. See [searching](using/search.md).
- *Touch panel.* Show a whiteboard on a shared screen without signing in a person. See [the touch panel](using/touch-panel.md).
- *Help in the app.* Every page has a Help link that opens the guide for that screen beside your work. Stewards and administrators have their own guides on the Help home page.
