## What this is for

Occasionally a post is wrong, off-topic or should not have been shared. As a steward you can remove other people's posts and replies. The author can always delete their own.

## Steps

1. Open the workspace on the **Noticeboard** tab and find the post or reply.
2. Choose **Remove** beside it. On your own posts the button says **Delete**.
3. A box asks for the reason. Type it plainly, for example "Posted in the wrong workspace".
4. Confirm the box.

## What happens next

The post disappears from the noticeboard straight away for everyone. Your name, the time and your reason are recorded so that others can see why. Files attached to the post still count towards the workspace's storage, and show up on the **Storage** tab as **Of those, attached to removed posts**.

## If something goes wrong

- If you leave the reason empty or cancel the box, nothing is removed.
- If **Remove** is missing, you are not a steward of this workspace, or it is archived.
- You cannot bring a removed post back from the screen. Ask the author to post it again.
- Chat messages have no remove button.
