## What this is for

The **Storage** tab shows what this workspace's own files take up. It helps you notice when the board or noticeboard is filling with large files, before the site runs short. It shows sizes and counts only, never file names or contents.

## Steps

1. Open the workspace and choose the **Storage** tab.
2. Read **Original files retained**. It gives the total size and the number of files.
3. Read **Of those, attached to removed posts**. These files stay stored even though their post is gone.
4. Read **Previews with recorded measurements** and **Previews with unknown measurements**. Unknown means a preview was never measured, not that it is empty.
5. Look at **Observed** for the time the figures were taken.
6. Choose **Refresh workspace usage** to measure again.

## What happens next

The figures cover this workspace only. Its parent and sibling workspaces are measured separately. You can use the numbers to decide whether to ask people to stop uploading large pictures, or to ask an administrator for advice.

## If something goes wrong

- **Workspace budget** says "Not configured". No limit is set for the workspace, so no limit is applied.
- Pending uploads are shown as not attributable until they are attached to something.
- Private notes and uploads that were never attached are not counted.
- The page says its figures are partial. Treat them as a guide, not as the exact disk size.
