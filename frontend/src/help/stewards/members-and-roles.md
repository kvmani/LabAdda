## What this is for

Everyone in a workspace can read what is shared there, so who belongs matters. Stewards of a group manage its members themselves.

## Before you start

You can add and remove people only in a group you steward. In a unit or project the **Members** tab is a list for reference, and an administrator changes who is in it. Members of sub-units can read and post in their parent unit as inherited members, but not the other way round.

## Steps

1. Open the workspace and choose the **Members** tab.
2. Read the table of **Name** and **Role**. Former staff are marked **Inactive**.
3. To add people, go to **Add colleagues**. In **Find colleagues**, type a name or username and choose a person. Repeat for each person.
4. Tick the box that says new members can read all existing posts, chat history and the board.
5. Choose the button that starts with **Add** and ends with **to group**.
6. To change a role, choose **Make steward** or **Make member** beside the person.
7. To remove someone, choose **Remove** beside them and confirm.

## What happens next

New members see the whole history of the group at once. A person you remove stops being able to open anything in the group straight away. Files they already downloaded stay with them.

## If something goes wrong

- If a colleague does not appear, check the spelling. Only active people are listed.
- If **Add** is greyed out, you have not ticked the box yet.
- You do not see **Remove** beside your own name. To leave, choose **Leave group** (see [handing over stewardship](handing-over-stewardship.md)).
- Archived groups cannot be changed. Choose **Unarchive group** first.
