## What this is for

A pinned post stays at the top of the noticeboard however many posts follow. An official notice is labelled and sends an alert to members, which suits rules, closures and booking changes.

## Steps

1. Open the workspace on the **Noticeboard** tab.
2. To pin a post, choose **Pin** at the top right of the post. The post moves up and shows **Pinned**.
3. To unpin it, choose **Unpin**.
4. To write an official notice, type your post as usual and tick **Official notice** below the box. Only stewards see this box.
5. Choose **Post**.

## What happens next

Pinned posts always show first. An official notice shows the **Official notice** label, and members who have not turned off official notice alerts receive a notification.

## If something goes wrong

- You cannot make an existing post official. Write a new post, and ask the author to delete the old one, or remove it yourself (see [removing a post](removing-a-post.md)).
- If you do not see **Pin**, you are not a steward here or the workspace is archived.
- Members who have switched off official notice alerts will not be notified, but they still see the notice on the board.
