## What this is for

A steward looks after a workspace so that it stays tidy and the right people can use it. This page lists what you can do and where. Being a steward lets you manage the workspace; it does not let you read anyone's private notes or other workspaces.

## Steps

1. Check you are a steward. Open the workspace and look under its name, where the line starts with **Steward**.
2. Use the **Noticeboard** tab to pin posts, mark official notices and remove posts that should not be there. See [pinning and official notices](pinning-and-official-notices.md) and [removing a post](removing-a-post.md).
3. Use the **Members** tab to see who belongs. In a group you can also add people, change roles and leave. See [adding and removing members](members-and-roles.md).
4. Use the **Members** tab to decide whether ordinary members can write. See [read-only and archived workspaces](read-only-and-archive.md).
5. To put the whiteboard back to an earlier state, open the **Whiteboard** tab and choose **Restore…**. Type the revision number you want, or 0 to clear the board. The current number is in the line beside the tools, after "rev".
6. Use the **Storage** tab to see how much space the workspace uses. See [workspace storage](workspace-storage.md).
7. Before you move on, hand the job to someone else. See [handing over stewardship](handing-over-stewardship.md).

## What happens next

Your changes take effect straight away for everyone in the workspace. Removals and role changes are recorded with your name.

## If something goes wrong

- If you cannot see a button described here, you may not be a steward of this workspace, or the workspace is archived.
- For a unit or a project, the list of members is managed by an administrator. Ask them to add or remove people there.
- Restoring the whiteboard replaces what everyone sees now. Check the revision number before you confirm.
