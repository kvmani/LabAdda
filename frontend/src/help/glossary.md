## What this is for

Short meanings for words you will see in LabAdda, in alphabetical order.

## Words

- *Administrator* is a person who looks after people and settings for the whole site. Being an administrator does not let someone read your messages or notes.
- *Group* is a private workspace you create for a few colleagues. Only the people you add can see it.
- *Member* is a person who has been added to a workspace and can take part in it.
- *Project* is a private workspace for a piece of work. It is separate from the unit structure.
- *Steward* is the member who looks after a workspace: adds people, tidies posts and decides who can write.
- *Unit* is a part of the organisation, such as a division or a section. People in a section can also see the division above it, but not the other way round.
- *Workspace* is a shared place with its own noticeboard, whiteboard, chat and tasks.
